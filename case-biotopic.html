01 — Overview

Biotopic — bridging museum and nature

Biotopic is a service design for Uppland's natural museum (Biotopia). The goal was broadening visitor diversity and enhancing interactions by seamlessly connecting physical exhibits with digital experiences.

02 — Problem

The problem statement

Biotopia faced limited visitor demographics and a weak connection between physical exhibitions and digital resources. Testing also revealed users rejected text-heavy quizzes and hesitated to download standalone apps.

85%
85% of users expressed a clear reluctance to download a standalone mobile app just for a one-time museum puzzle game.
82%
82% of current visitors are families with children under 10 — a severe lack of engagement from young adults and teenagers.
03 — Solution

A frictionless physical-to-digital ecosystem

We created a frictionless ecosystem. Visitors engage with physical posters, play a web-based pixel-art game (no download required), and use the Biotopic app's AI-Lens to explore nature — culminating in an in-person museum reward.

The Biotopic ecosystem — from poster to app to museum reward
04 — Systems

System map & interaction flows

The service touches three surfaces — a web activity, the mobile AI-Lens, and a backend portal for museum staff. Mapping how they hand off to each other was central to keeping the experience seamless.

System map connecting physical, web, and app touchpoints
Interaction flow across the ecosystem
05 — Process

Design process

The research made the friction points unmistakable, and the refinement removed each barrier to entry in turn.

Research

Observed that demographics heavily skewed toward families (82%) and identified a weak link between physical exhibits and digital resources.

Testing

Revealed major friction: 85% refused standalone downloads, 73% abandoned text-heavy quizzes, and 65% distrusted QR codes over privacy.

Refinement

Built a frictionless ecosystem using agile methods — a pixel-art web game and a visual AI-Lens to bridge the physical-digital gap.

Design process — research, testing, and refinement
06 — Features

Feature breakdown

Web-Activity — frictionless entry via poster QR codes leading to an engaging pixel-art game. AI-Lens App — a mobile tool letting users capture, identify, and map local wildlife sightings. Backend Portal — an AI-powered system helping museum staff moderate content and create campaigns.

Biotopia Tracker — the web-to-app onboarding reward loop
AI-Lens — wildlife identification, maps, and guided walks
07 — Outcome

Conclusion

By blending physical triggers with digital utility, Biotopic bridges the museum-to-nature gap — a scalable, sustainable model for community engagement without imposing heavy initial download barriers.
