01 — Overview

Real-time transparency for lift projects

Schindler One is a dedicated mobile application designed to enhance the overall customer experience. It provides real-time tracking and updates on lift projects supplied by the Schindler company — giving customers one clear place to follow their project from order to installation.

02 — Problem

The problem statement

Schindler lacked a centralized system for lift installations. This lack of transparency caused customer confusion, uncertainty, and dissatisfaction regarding project timelines, delays, and potential building disruptions.

78%
Analyzing 10,000+ internal complaints revealed 78% stemmed from poor communication.
22%
The remaining 22% stemmed from transparency issues and delays.
03 — Solution

A guided, real-time experience

The solution centralizes the entire lift-project lifecycle into an intuitive mobile app — surfacing order details, manufacturing and installation status, handover, and service requests, so customers always know exactly where their project stands.

Solution overview and the signup experience
04 — Process

Design process

The work moved from deep research into a communication problem, through comprehensive flows for complex industrial processes, and into a clean, brand-aligned visual system.

Research

Analyzed 10,000+ internal complaints — identifying communication gaps as the primary issue — and built detailed personas for both B2B facility managers and B2C homeowners.

Testing

Developed and evaluated comprehensive user flows, ensuring complex multi-step processes like manufacturing, shipping, and installation were easy to navigate.

Refinement

Designed high-fidelity interfaces in an industrial-yet-modern aesthetic — Frutiger LT Std and Schindler's signature red — distilling heavy data into a clean visual timeline.

Design process — research, testing, and refinement
05 — IA & Flow

User flow & information architecture

The architecture spans onboarding and account creation, a home dashboard, and detailed views for orders, product and manufacturing status, installation, handover, payments, warranty, and a feedback loop.
