01 — Overview

One stop for health & wellness

BlissLife is an app that helps people access resources and products for health and wellness — bringing diet plans, workouts, practitioner appointments, and e-commerce into a single experience.

02 — Problem

The problem statement

There's no one-stop solution where people can access all health and wellness resources and buy products for their diet and wellness plans. Spreading these across multiple apps makes accessing data complex and gets in the way of reaching goals.

The core idea was to combine the critical aspects users rely on daily — e-commerce, health goals, appointment booking, and diet plans — into one coherent experience.
03 — Approach

Design approach & objectives

Rather than force users to juggle separate apps for fitness, diet, and shopping, BlissLife unifies them. The experience is built around what people use every day: booking practitioners, following diet and workout regimes, and buying recommended products in one place.

Design process, onboarding illustrations, and a look at the workout experience
04 — Research

User research & personas

Research explored what people need in a health & wellness app, the resources they use to learn about health, and the products they'd want in a wellness store. An empathy map surfaced the frustration of "having to use multiple apps for these."

06 — IA & Flow

User journey & design style

The architecture connects Appointments, a personalized Dashboard, Diets, Workouts, and E-commerce — with practitioner chat, recommended articles, and product checkout. The visual system uses a Branding SF display face with a bright blue accent and a clean, modern palette.
