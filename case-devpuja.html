01 — Overview

Booking pandits, made simple

DevPuja is an intuitive website that simplifies booking pandits (priests) for religious functions — poojas, weddings, last rites, and ceremonies like janeu. The platform bridges the gap between tradition and modern convenience through a seamless online experience.

02 — Problem

The problem statement

Families often struggle to organize traditional religious ceremonies — limited access to authentic pandits, diverse regional practices, and no standardized booking process. Existing methods are time-consuming, rely on unreliable references, and lack transparency in pricing and services. Many users, especially younger generations, are also unfamiliar with rituals and their requirements.

There's a pressing need for a convenient, reliable, and culturally sensitive platform that simplifies the process while preserving the authenticity and sanctity of Indian religious traditions.
03 — Approach

Design approach & objectives

DevPuja lets users browse and book verified pandits by region, language, and ritual expertise. It offers transparent pricing, customisable ceremony options, and add-ons like puja materials and decor. With a friendly interface, multi-language support, and emergency booking, it makes organising religious functions seamless while preserving cultural authenticity and trust.

06 — IA & Flow

User journey & design style

The architecture spans Home, About, Services (online, matrimonial, and janeu services), Contact, and FAQ — plus onboarding, preferences, and settings. The visual system pairs a bold Gilroy display face with a warm orange accent drawn from ceremonial fire.
